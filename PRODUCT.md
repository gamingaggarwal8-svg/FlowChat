# FlowChat

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Rakesh and other WhatsApp-first Indian textile distributors managing customers, suppliers and godown stock. They need quick decisions during a busy trading day, with laptop and phone access.

## Product Purpose
Connect conversations, invoices, payments, purchase orders and inventory into an evidence-backed daily operations view. Inventory and growth lead; supplier delivery commitments and cash risk remain close alongside. The user confirmed this priority on 14 November 2026.

## Positioning
Prepared Hinglish conversation annotations connect customer and owner payment promises with delivery promises, deterministic reconciliation and stock decisions. Calculated answers expose original business evidence.

## Operating Context
Demo date is fixed at 2026-11-14. All supplied data is synthetic. The Next.js app reads /data; the static demo bundles the same React UI with prepared source records. Browser-local imports, catalog edits, approval drafts and reviewed transcripts persist locally.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Keep every existing workflow: customer balances and evidence, suppliers and exports, inventory filters and catalog editor, stock-cover scenarios, charts, natural-language keyword analysis, promise tracking, chat review, voice transcripts, bargaining and approval drafts. Money and stock math remain deterministic. Nothing auto-sends. Below-floor prices require owner review and floor prices never appear in customer replies. Voice-file upload is playback only; automatic transcription is unconnected. Browser microphone recognition is browser-dependent. No API key is needed for the dashboard. Live AI and forecasting must never be claimed as connected.

## Brand Commitments
FlowChat; tagline Chat se cash tak. A light, professional, colourful working interface with distinct customer, supplier and inventory surfaces. User explicitly requested a frontend redesign using Impeccable, not a change to the business logic.

## Evidence on Hand
Synthetic 12-customer, 7-supplier, 10-product dataset; expected metrics and extraction fixtures; deterministic analytics tests; source chats; prior user screenshots of customer ledger. Those screenshots predate later UI changes and are not current-render evidence.

## Product Principles
- Put the next inventory decision before decorative summaries.
- Keep on-hand stock separate from incoming quantities and scenarios.
- Make financial evidence reachable from the number.
- Distinguish uncertain promises and prepared annotations from verified payment records.
- Owner approval controls every outgoing message and purchase commitment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable Indian-format currency, responsive tables and charts, labelled controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support and clear empty/error states. Hinglish transcript text must remain exact source evidence.
