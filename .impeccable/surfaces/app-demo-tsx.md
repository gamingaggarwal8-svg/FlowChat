## Challenger verdicts
Variable-font specimen: declined; the task requires readable data hierarchy, not a giant letter. Keep typographic consistency and alignment discipline.
Jet-age ticket wallet: competitive in precise commitments, loses on everyday textile operation familiarity; keep original-versus-revised dates visibly distinct.
Centre-rail reference setting: declined; column convention cannot replace the app shell. Keep rigorous row alignment and strong evidence relationships.
Timetable slide rack: declined; skewed containers and uniform type size reduce operational scanability. Keep unambiguous state marks.
Japanese high-density site: declined; tiny type recreates the incumbent's main defect. Keep meaningful information density without small labels.
Character-goods catalog: declined; merchandising styling should not displace the daily operations task. Keep distinct category identities, not decorative character art.
